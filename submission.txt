TASK 12 - Eigen solution of (4*I+A3)*y = w
Answer: 46 iterations; final relative residual = 2.7198720144561966e-11.
Solver: Eigen BiCGSTAB.
Preconditioner: DiagonalPreconditioner.
Prescribed tolerance: 1e-10; maximum iterations: 2000.
Initial guess: y0 = w/4, supplied through solveWithGuess.
An independent check gives ||w-(4*I+A3)*y||_2 / ||w||_2
= 2.7198721953538819e-11.
The absolute residual is 1.2270642132934253e-06.
The verified relative residual satisfies the prescribed tolerance.

Justification of the initial guess:
The diagonal of M = 4*I+A3 is D = 4*I. Therefore y0 = D^(-1)*w = w/4
solves the diagonal approximation and is one Jacobi step starting from zero.
The initial residual is r0 = w-M*y0 = -A3*w/4.
In a controlled comparison with the same seed-42 input, matrix, solver,
preconditioner and tolerance, this choice reduced the initial relative
residual from 1 to approximately 0.390 and the iteration count from 48 to 46.
The diagonal preconditioner is uniform scaling by 1/4; it does not improve
the condition number. The initial guess and the preconditioner serve
different roles.

IMPLEMENTATION AND SOLVER CHOICES
Both systems are nonsymmetric, so BiCGSTAB is an appropriate Krylov solver;
ordinary Conjugate Gradient's symmetric positive definite assumptions do
not apply. ILU(0) provides a sparse approximation to the factors of A2.
The Eigen system uses the simple diagonal preconditioner and the justified
w/4 initial guess described above.
Sparse operators use row-major storage for repeated matrix-vector products.
Pixel vectorization is row-wise, independently of matrix storage order.
Convolution follows the assignment's unflipped kernels and zero padding.
PNG output clips to [0, 255] and rounds to bytes. Negative edge responses
appear black; solution images retain their computed intensity scale.
